PRIVACY POLICY

Signal Run: Ghosts Journey · Last updated 9 November 2026

Signal Run does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data.

What we collect

Nothing. The game contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking of any kind, no accounts and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This includes:

  • Your high score, level, rank and in-game currency
  • Guns, attachments and skins you have unlocked
  • Your chosen pilot name and settings
  • Daily task progress and achievements

This information never leaves your device. It is not uploaded anywhere, it is not readable by us, and it is not shared with anyone. Deleting the app removes it.

Internet access

The game itself makes no network requests and plays entirely offline. The one exception is the optional Game Center leaderboard described below, which connects only when you use it.

Game Center

On iPhone and iPad the game offers optional Apple Game Center leaderboards for your best score and your best wave. If you are signed in to Game Center, your best score and best wave are sent to Apple so they can appear in the leaderboard, alongside the Game Center nickname you chose with Apple. This is handled entirely by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; we never receive or store your Game Center name or ID, and no other information is sent. You can sign out of Game Center at any time in your device Settings, and the rest of the game carries on working exactly as before.

Notifications

The game can send you optional reminders — for example when your daily tasks reset. These are scheduled on your own device by your device, not sent from a server, and we receive nothing when one is shown. You can refuse them when asked, or turn them off at any time from Settings inside the game or in your device Settings.

Third parties

No advertising networks, no analytics providers, no social media integrations and no cloud services. The only outside service is Apple Game Center, used solely for the optional leaderboard described above.

Children

Because the game collects no data at all, it collects no data from children. It is safe for players of any age in this respect.

Your rights

There is no data for us to show you, correct, export or delete, because we never receive any. The save data on your device is entirely under your control, and the game includes a Reset Progress option that erases it.

Changes to this policy

If the game ever changes in a way that affects this policy, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.
